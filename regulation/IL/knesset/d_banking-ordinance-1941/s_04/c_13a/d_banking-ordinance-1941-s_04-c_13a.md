# סעיף 13א — חשבון משותף וכספת משותפת

Joint Account and Joint Safe

## תוכן (Content)

**(א)** Where a banking corporation received funds, securities, or other negotiable documents in a joint account regarding which it was stipulated with the banking corporation that upon the death of one account holder, the surviving account holder (or person acting on their behalf by law) shall be entitled to continue operating the account — such stipulation shall remain valid, with respect to the relationship between the banking corporation and the account holders, even after the death.

**(ב)** The provisions of subsection (a) shall apply, mutatis mutandis, to a safe that a banking corporation rented to two or more persons under the aforementioned condition, without derogating from the provisions of section 13(b) of the Estate Tax Law, 5709-1949.
