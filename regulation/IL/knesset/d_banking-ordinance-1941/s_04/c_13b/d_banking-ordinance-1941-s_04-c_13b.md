# סעיף 13ב — פקדונות ללא תנועה

Dormant Deposits

> **This is the key statutory provision** establishing the legal framework for handling dormant deposits in Israeli banking. The Banking Order (Dormant Deposits) 2000 and BOI Directive 436 implement the rules set out here.

## תוכן (Content)

**(א)** Contact obligation — A banking corporation shall attempt to contact the owner of a dormant deposit close to the maturity date of the deposit. For a demand deposit, the maturity date is deemed to be the day on which the deposit became a dormant deposit (i.e., 10 months since the last instruction from the owner).

**(ב)** Securities — This section also applies to funds received in respect of securities held in custody at a banking corporation. The obligation to attempt contact applies close to the date of receipt of such funds.

**(ג)** Investment obligation — If the banking corporation fails to contact the deposit owner within two months, it shall invest the funds to the owner's credit in the manner prescribed by the Governor, with the approval of the Minister of Finance and the Knesset Finance Committee.

**(ד)** Minimum threshold — This section does not apply to a dormant deposit in an amount not exceeding one hundred shekels, or another amount set by the Governor with the approval of the Minister of Finance and the Knesset Finance Committee. However, a banking corporation may voluntarily act in accordance with this section even for deposits below the threshold amount.

## Key Obligations Summary

| Sub-section | Obligation | Timeframe |
|-------------|-----------|-----------|
| (א) | Contact deposit owner | Close to maturity date |
| (ג) | Invest unclaimed funds per Governor's rules | After 2 months of failed contact |
| (ד) | Minimum threshold exemption | Per Governor's order |
