# פיקדונות ללא תנועה — Dormant Deposits

Sections 13-13ב of the Banking Ordinance 1941.

This is the key section establishing the legal framework for handling dormant deposits in Israeli banking. It covers early repayment of housing loans, joint accounts, and the core obligations regarding dormant deposits.
