# סעיף 13 — פדיון מוקדם של הלוואה

Early Repayment of Housing Loan

## תוכן (Content)

**(א)** A person who received a loan from a banking corporation for the purpose of purchasing a residential dwelling or with a mortgage on a residential dwelling may, notwithstanding any contrary agreement, repay it before the scheduled repayment date under these conditions:

1. The early repayment amount shall not be less than 10% of the original loan amount or 10% of the remaining balance plus accrued interest and indexation differentials, whichever is higher.
2. The banking corporation may condition early repayment on payment of a fee at rates and conditions set by the Governor by order, after consulting the Committee and with approval of the Minister of Finance and the Knesset Finance Committee.

**(ב)** The provisions of subsection (a) also apply to a loan where the banking corporation assigned its rights to a non-banking entity.

**(ג)** A banking corporation shall not assign a loan of the type described in subsection (a) unless arrangements have been made with the assigning banking corporation or another corporation ensuring that service to the customer will not be materially impaired.
