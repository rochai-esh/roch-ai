# הגבלת השימוש בתואר "בנק" — Restriction on use of "Bank" title

Sections 4-4א of the Banking Ordinance 1941.

## סעיף 4 — הגבלת השימוש בתואר "בנק"

Prohibits any person or group from using the word "bank" (בנק) or any derivative thereof in their business name without the Governor's consent, except for licensed banks, foreign banks, mortgage banks, investment financing banks, and business promotion banks.

Key provisions:
- **4(1)** — Prohibition on use of "bank" without Governor's consent
- **4(2)** — Penalty: fine of up to 500 shekels per day of continuing offense
- **4(3)** — Governor may order removal of "bank" from business name after consultation with committee and hearing
- **4(4)** — Governor shall not exercise power under 4(3) against licensed entities unless revocation order under 8ד or 8יד issued
- **4(5)** — Repealed (בוטל)
- **4(6)** — If entity fails to comply, Governor may choose a name and notify the registrar

## סעיף 4א — שמות העלולים להטעות (Misleading Names)

If a non-banking entity uses a business name that, while not containing "bank", implies it engages in activities reserved for banking corporations under sections 13 or 21 of the Banking (Licensing) Law, the Governor may — after consultation and hearing — order removal of such misleading words within at least 28 days.

Non-compliance treated same as unauthorized use of "bank" in name.
