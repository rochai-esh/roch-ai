# סמכויות הנגיד והמפקח — Powers of Governor and Supervisor

Sections 5-12 of the Banking Ordinance 1941.

This section consolidates the supervisory and enforcement powers of the Bank of Israel Governor and the Supervisor of Banks. It covers appointment of the Supervisor, advisory committee, stability measures, and reporting obligations.
